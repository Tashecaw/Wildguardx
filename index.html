Why Protect Animals?

Many animals are in danger because of pollution, hunting, and losing their habitats. We can help by learning about these problems and making better choices.

Animals We Should Protect

Tiger

Tigers are endangered because of hunting and habitat loss.

Elephant

Elephants need safe places to live and protection from poaching.

Sea Turtle

Sea turtles are affected by ocean pollution and fishing.

Whale

Whales need clean oceans and protection from human activities.

How Can We Help?

  • Pick up litter and keep the environment clean.
  • Learn about endangered animals.
  • Do not support illegal wildlife trading.
  • Teach other people why animals matter.
